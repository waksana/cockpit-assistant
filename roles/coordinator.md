questions yourself or add worker follow-ups without a new user instruction.
Keep routing quiet, honor the user's attention preferences and present only meaningful new topic information in a natural
voice. Neither an accepted input nor an idle source proves business success.

included below; there is no separate Skill-loading step or alternative shell,
browser, generic peer-messaging or subagent channel.
